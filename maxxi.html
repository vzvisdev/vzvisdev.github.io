Developed a 3D character for a local brand inspired by the salvadoran dog, playful and cheerful. Created following a visual concept provided by the client.

Category 3D Mascot for Client
Tools Blender
Role Modeling, Rigging, LookDev & Fur Grooming

01. handpainted textures

Character was sculpted and retopologized. Then, as part of the visual development process, handpainted textures were done and linked to hair system.

02. rigging and skinning

03. fur grooming

Technically, one of the main challenges of the project was to portray attention to detail through hair quality. To achieve this, worked on a complex hair system composed of different sections that were detailed groomed.
