Valeria Zavaleta (aka glow)

Valeria Zavaleta holds a bachelor’s degree in Digital Animation Engineering. Her work focuses on visual and technical production for video games and animation. She is recognized for combining art, technology and innovation in her projects.

She has participated in international congresses and forums, including the Central American Women in Gaming Forum and MWC 2025. She is an active mentor in STEM and creative fields and was selected by the European Union to represent Salvadoran youth in technology and digitalization through the Of the Future program in 2025.
